Travel Survival 207

Taking a Taxi Back to the Hotel

1/6

Last time, you ordered food and bought souvenirs in Kyoto. Today sightseeing is finished. Find a taxi, tell the driver where to go, ask about time and price, and pay when you arrive.

Today's Target

  • Find the taxi stand.
  • Tell the driver your destination.
  • Ask about travel time and price.
  • Stop, pay, and thank the driver.

First, find the taxi stand. Reuse the familiar pattern ___ はどこですか.

Travel Question

タクシー乗のり場ばはどこですか。

Where is the taxi stand?

Straight

まっすぐ行いってください。

Right

右みぎに曲まがってください。

Left

左ひだりに曲まがってください。

Logic: [Destination] + まで + お願いします

Tell the Driver

Hoteru made onegai shimasu.

運転手うんてんしゅ

どちらまでですか。

Where are you going?

旅行者りょこうしゃ

この住所じゅうしょまでお願ねがいします。

To this address, please.

Ask about time or an approximate price. Listen for the number and the final unit: 分 or 円.

Time

どのくらいかかりますか。

About how long will it take?

Price

いくらぐらいですか。

About how much will it cost?
