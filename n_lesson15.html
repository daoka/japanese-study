Travel Survival 206

Cafe and Souvenir Shop in Kyoto

1/6

Last time, you asked for directions in Kyoto. Today you arrive near a sightseeing place, enter a cafe or souvenir shop, ask what something is, ask the price, order, and ask if photos are okay.

Today's Target

  • Ask what an item is.
  • Ask for a recommendation.
  • Ask the price and order politely.
  • Ask if taking a photo is okay.

Review Stack

Use short questions first. You do not need long sentences to start a real shop conversation.

What is this?

これは何なんですか。

What is this?

Recommendation

おすすめは何なんですか。

What do you recommend?

Reaction

いいですね。

That is nice.

Your Order

これ を 1つください 。

Kore o hitotsu kudasai.

After choosing something, ask the price. If needed, ask whether a card can be used.

Price

いくらですか。

How much is it?

Payment

カードは使つかえますか。

Can I use a card?

Listen

500円えんです。

It is 500 yen.

Yes

はい、使つかえます。

Yes, you can use it.

No

すみません、現金げんきんだけです。

Sorry, cash only.

At temples, cafes, and shops, ask before taking a photo. Treat this as one useful phrase.

Permission Phrase

写真しゃしんを
撮とってもいいですか。

May I take a photo?

OK

はい、いいですよ。

Yes, that is okay.

Not OK

すみません、だめです。

Sorry, no.

Conversation Focus

If you hear いいですよ, you can take a photo. If you hear だめです, say わかりました and stop.
